# Product scope

HOL0CRON is a product-building hackathon, not a timed algorithm-solving competition. Its mission is tools that improve independent competitive-programming reasoning: training, learning analytics, problem setting, test engineering, integrity, and judging quality.

Confirmed: 22 December 2026; Campus 25, Lecture Halls, KIIT University, Bhubaneswar; KIIT undergraduate years 1–3; exactly three members. Organizers: AlgoZenith KIIT Chapter, KodeWreck, School of Computer Engineering, KIIT University.

Participant journey: discover mission → team identification → three-member manifest → six unchecked acknowledgments → full review → committed registration → pending-review team ticket → organizer approval → admission check-in. Recovery is available through a saved private key.

Organizer journey: individual login → search/filter → review private roster → verify enrollment externally → approve/flag/revoke according to role → verify scanned ticket → explicit atomic check-in. Administrators export the filtered summary view (up to 200 rows); participant contacts are not included in that export.

Unconfirmed settings stay explicit. No invented fees, prizes, deadlines, selection rounds, sponsors, or contacts. Registration defaults closed until approved policies and domain configuration exist. No email delivery, camera scanning, SSO, self-service roster editing, or automated enrollment lookup is claimed.
